One of the core principles of LG-TLA is reducing foods that contribute to chronic inflammation, excess calorie intake, and poor metabolic health. These "Red Light" foods are best avoided because t[...]
Examples of Red Light foods include:

- Deep-fried foods: French fries, pakoras, samosas, kachoris, and other fried snacks control remains important because it is high in fat and calories ([Qin et al. 2021](#ref-Qin2021FriedHeart)).
- Processed and packaged foods: Potato chips, patties, packaged breads, biscuits, and many commercially prepared sauces ([Dai et al. 2024](#ref-Dai2024)).
- Sugary treats and confectionery: Candy, chocolates, toffees, cakes, pastries, ice cream, and other desserts high in added sugar ([Lara-Castor et al. 2025](#ref-LaraCastor2025SSB)).
- Sugar-sweetened beverages: Soft drinks, sodas, and other carbonated beverages ([Reynolds et al. 2019](#ref-Reynolds2019Fiber)).
- Milk-based caffeinated beverages: Avoid Tea and coffee prepared with milk and added sugar.
- Red meat: Frequent consumption of processed meats and large amounts of red meat should be limited.
- Alcoholic beverages: Alcohol provides little nutritional value and is associated with numerous adverse health outcomes.
- 🔴 Red lifestyle habit points to addictive substances that needs to be shunned.
